Billboard — Deploy

Deploy and verify the Billboard contracts on L1 (Ethereum) and L2 (Aztec).

Loads a 56MB bundle + 1.7MB contract artifact. Be patient.

⚠️ Alpha experimental software. Not meant for production use.

Configuration

Deposit & Cooldown Parameters

Configure the minimum deposit and the posting cooldown at that deposit. Cooldown is rounded up to whole seconds. At 10× min deposit, cooldown is 10× shorter.

Censor (optional)

Set a censor address that can flag posts as immoral. Flagged posts are hidden and the poster's cooldown is multiplied by K. Set to 0x0 to disable.

Moderation Policy

The censorship policy text, stored on-chain. The censor can update it later. Max 1488 bytes.

Load both wallets above, then click Deploy.
