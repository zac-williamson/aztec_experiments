2. Deposit ETH

No active deposit found on L1. Enter an amount and click the button below to deposit ETH into the L1 portal. The deposit will be automatically claimed on L2 once the L1→L2 message is ingested (~5-10 min). Cooldown = 1 hour × (0.001 ETH ÷ your deposit).

You have an active deposit on L1. Processing starts automatically — no button click needed. The app will find your deposit, check its L2 status, and either claim it on L2 (if not yet claimed) or proceed to claim your ETH on L1 (if already withdrawn).

4. Withdraw ETH on L2

Consume your deposit note on L2 and send an L2→L1 message. After the epoch proof lands on L1, you'll claim your ETH in the next step.

5. Claim ETH on L1

Consume the L2→L1 message via the portal to receive your ETH. This waits for the epoch proof to land on L1 (~40 min), then calls portal.withdraw() on L1. Works even if you withdrew in a previous session — it scans L2 blocks to find your withdrawal tx.
