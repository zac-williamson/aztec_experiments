Billboard — Censor Panel

Flag posts as immoral and manage censorship rights.

Loads a 56MB bundle + 1.7MB contract artifact. Be patient.

⚠️ Alpha experimental software. Not meant for production use.

Configuration

Enter the L1 Portal address (from the deploy app), then load the censor's Aztec wallet below. The L2 contract address is derived from the portal on chain. No ETH wallet needed — the censor only operates on L2.

Censor Status

Flag Post as Immoral

Flagged posts are hidden from the billboard view by default, and the poster's cooldown is multiplied by K.

Transfer Censor Rights

Transfer censorship authority to another Aztec address. Only the current censor can do this.
