Fund private fees

Bridge your AZTEC tokens into a private balance. Your transactions spend that balance through a shared, ownerless contract.

You need AZTEC tokens and ETH for Ethereum gas in your connected wallet. This page does not exchange tokens.

1. Load your wallets

2. Deposit AZTEC

Recovery metadata is saved in this browser before submission. Download a copy and keep your wallet backup.

3. Claim your private balance

Once the bridge message is available, prove a private claim. Its fee comes from the deposit. This deducts the configured maximum fee; unused gas is not refunded.
