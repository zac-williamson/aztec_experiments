Fee Juice -- Deposit & Claim

Bridge L1 AZTEC to L2 Fee Juice on Aztec, then claim it in-browser. Runs the full PXE stack (no server, no workers).

Loads a 58MB bundle. Be patient -- it takes 10-30 seconds to parse.

⚠️ Alpha experimental software. Not meant for production use.

1. Load Wallets & Check Node

Load or generate wallets below. You can load existing JSON files or generate new ones (which will be downloaded to your computer). Node connection and balance check run automatically once all are loaded.

2. Deposit AZTEC → Fee Juice (L1)

Approve and deposit AZTEC via the FeeJuicePortal on Ethereum mainnet. A deterministic claim secret is generated from your browser wallet signature + nonce. The button below connects your browser wallet, approves, and deposits in one flow.

Recover a past deposit: Already deposited and lost the claim secret? Enter the L1 deposit tx hash below. The app will derive the claim secret from your wallet signature, verify it against the on-chain deposit, check L2 inclusion, and take you straight to the claim step.

3. Claim Fee Juice on L2

Claims the L1→L2 message in-browser using the full PXE stack. The app verifies the message is available before starting the 14-minute proving step. Fields are auto-filled from the deposit above, or you can enter them manually / auto-fetch from an L1 tx hash.
