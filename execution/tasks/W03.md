## Work

1. Model each deposit, claim, post, flag, screen, withdrawal and L1-claim stage with durable IDs and validated receipts.
2. Handle pending, success, revert, drop, unknown and reorganized outcomes explicitly.
3. Recover after long absence using persisted provenance and complete event queries rather than a 500-block window.

## Acceptance

- **W03-A01** — Application reverts never show success or complete moderation jobs.
- **W03-A02** — Reload/crash at every stage resumes without duplicate deposit, claim, post or withdrawal.
- **W03-A03** — Long-absent withdrawal recovery works beyond 500 blocks without requiring an unstored transaction hash.
- **W03-A04** — Stale proofs are regenerated where required; RPC timeouts and reorgs reconcile durable state instead of guessing success.
