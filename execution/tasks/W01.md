## Work

1. Implement canonical ownerless private FeeJuice payments with no issuer, coupons or offchain fee operator.
2. Fund and recover private credit through the protocol portal; qualify cold-start and subsequent application transactions with real proofs.
3. Assess funding and transaction observations, explicit maximum-fee charging, insufficient balance, replay and ambiguous submission behavior.

## Acceptance

- **W01-A01** — Ordinary posting does not expose the author account as public fee payer; funding amount/timing and cold-start linkage are explicitly assessed against the privacy model.
- **W01-A02** — Fee/RPC/operator observations are captured and assessed against the declared privacy model.
- **W01-A03** — Insufficient private credit or unsupported fees fail explicitly without public author-payment fallback.
- **W01-A04** — Users fund and recover their own private fee credit without an issuer; maximum-fee charging and replay protection are verified without collecting author identities.
