## Can Ready be reused?

**Not safely with the current disposable harness after cleanup.** `test-c01-real-network.mjs` generates fresh L1 identity in the worker, starts fresh Anvil, and creates fresh account preparation; `c01-real-node.mjs` places node state below the owned temporary directory. Worker teardown stops Anvil/node, and the parent deletes that tree only after descendant cleanup. Secrets and prepared account state are deliberately not exported. A previous Ready receipt/proof belongs to its original rollup, portal, board, canonical chain history and verifier domain. Its sanitized evidence cannot activate a fresh portal or stand in for that chain's actual finalized state. Reusing verified compiler binaries/CRS is supported; reusing the prior application settlement in a fresh chain is not.

Avoiding repeated Ready would require a **new explicit staged harness** that keeps the same supervised local chain and private account state alive between separately bounded stages, rechecks source/binary identities and canonical successful Ready receipt, actual finalized Rollup proof/event coverage and live enabled portal before deposit, and owns final cleanup across all stages. Existing generic settlement already supports `startProver:false` and detecting an already-proven target, but it assumes the real live node/state still exists; it is not a restoration mechanism. Such lifecycle work is broader than changing a budget and is unqualified today. Do not use fake roots, import old proof observations as live state, or omit the actual Ready activation just to shorten a repeat. For a timeout of this run, a fresh full journey with the declared larger bound is the smallest trustworthy next step.
