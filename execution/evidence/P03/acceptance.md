## Review and limits

Independent AI cross-reviews of shell/receipt and root history tests are preserved.
AI reviews do not satisfy the external audit gate. The history agent's automated
content flag and a Foundry macOS proxy discovery failure were recorded accurately;
root handled ordinary local history tests and offline Foundry resolved its failure.
No article was removed and no missing test was silently counted as passing.

The new tests expose failure semantics for future repairs. W03 and C04 must replace
known-bad receipt/history assertions with desired successful behavior; retain their
normal controls and historical documentation. P03 does not freeze obsolete defects
as intended product behavior. Supported V5 migration and further implementation
remain ahead of deployment, audit, proof, soak and official clearance gates.
