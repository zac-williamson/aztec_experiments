# Requirement provenance and delivery boundaries

## Explicit user direction

Production-ready anonymous message board; no backwards-compatibility obligation;
ownerless private user-funded fees with coupon service removed; actual application
proofs and official accelerated local settlement controls, not network epoch
proving; tests below ten minutes; bounded delegated autonomous execution; commit
and push the fork; V5 engineering with intended V6 production transition.
Deployment, spending real funds and contacting third parties need their own authority.

## Engineering qualification defaults

The exact 14-day soak, three desktop browser matrix, 30-sample latency campaigns,
1,000-post history workload and 2%/5% model error limits were proposed engineering
choices. They are not separate explicit user demands or universal definitions of
production readiness. Existing evidence must continue to state their actual results.
They remain the recorded release baseline until a reasoned revision identifies
its assurance, product-support and schedule effects. Never change a threshold
merely because a candidate failed.

Correct collateral accounting, authentic notes, private fee routing, safe recovery
and honest product claims are substantive application obligations. Their specific
test implementations can improve without weakening those obligations. External
qualified review and sustained operational evidence remain release requirements;
internal AI review is not an audit.

## Practical delivery plan

1. Application lane: isolate failed hostile-witness test, finish privacy analysis
   and genuine browser lifecycle/recovery.
2. Operations lane: monitoring, actionable alerts and reproducible recovery drills
   using existing moderation interfaces. Model-quality approval is separate.
3. Release preparation lane: architecture/reviewer scope and existing evidence
   inventory now; freeze a complete candidate only after internal qualification.

The model needs a suitable evaluated candidate and independently reviewed held-out
labels. Network clearance, operator ownership and reviewer engagement are explicit
release dependencies, not reasons to stop unrelated engineering. The current
14-day final soak imposes a minimum of 14 actual days after candidate eligibility;
no calendar completion date is credible before those dependencies are resolved.

## Verification cost control

Keep one source-bound result per meaningful scenario and reuse unaffected evidence
with a recorded source reconciliation. Do not rerun a full bridge journey to debug
a pure cache/proxy function. Use the smallest discriminating regression first,
then one integrated proof run. Record attempts and reassess after two expensive
failures per hypothesis. Final candidate qualification still verifies all required
behavior. Do not alter historical evidence or fingerprint semantics retroactively.
